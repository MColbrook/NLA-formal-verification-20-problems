## Palomar-specific publication facts

Palomar requires one unambiguous standard SPDX root licence matching `project.license`; custom or mixed root declarations fail. Authors and responsible maintainers must be humans. Material AI roles belong in automation, with truthful review and provenance disclosures. The submitter must be a responsible author/maintainer or have approval from one; repository write access alone is insufficient. Registration makes source and records permanent public history. These are distinct from copyright permission. See the official [submission policy](https://github.com/PalomarRegistry/PalomarPolicy/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#25-repository-licence) and [protocol](https://github.com/PalomarRegistry/PalomarPolicy/blob/main/docs/specification.md).

For this package:

1. The owner confirmed Matthew J. Colbrook and George Stepaniants as formalization authors and responsible maintainers for this release. Retain source copyright and bibliographic attribution regardless; these are different kinds of credit. This new confirmation, rather than historical copied maintainer metadata, is the naming basis.
2. Disclose substantial OpenAI Codex assistance in proof construction, source correspondence review and packaging. Historical exact model identifiers, hardware, cost and wall time must be reported only where recorded; do not reconstruct unknown facts. Acknowledge distinct AI review as AI review. Do not claim independent human review or tool-author endorsement from existing AI reports.
3. Describe the result as source-based formalization, with the manuscript and original problem collection separately identified. Put earlier Lean developments in `related_formalizations`, and explain copied, ported and extended material. Preserve actual mathematical attribution for each problem rather than attributing every earlier result to a single paper author.
4. State verification scope accurately: existing component receipts and the earlier combined replay are historical evidence; a fresh current combined replay was cancelled by the user. No new kernel replay, external submission, or GitHub publication was performed for this report.
